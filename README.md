# Cuarto Oscuro — the darkroom, resurrected

**Kiroween 2026 · Category: Resurrection**

Digital photography killed the darkroom ritual: waiting, red light, trays,
contact sheets marked with a grease pencil. *Cuarto Oscuro* brings it back in the
browser: load your photos, watch them *develop* slowly in the tray, print a
contact sheet and mark your picks with a red grease pencil.

## Run
Open `src/index.html` in a browser (no build, no server, nothing leaves your machine).
